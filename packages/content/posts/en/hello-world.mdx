## Hello world

This is the first post in my personal garden. Writing in both Vietnamese and English, each
variant sharing the same `translationKey` so the two pages can link to one another.

I write here to keep track of what I learn about the web, developer tooling, and everyday
life. The goal throughout is reading comfort: generous type, high contrast, and a dimmed
warm dark theme instead of pure black.

### Why a garden

Not a magazine, not a social network — just a place to keep notes and revise them over time.
A few working principles:

- Write in both Vietnamese and English, paired by a shared translation key.
- Tag each post so it can be filtered later.
- Keep everything static: fast, durable, and dependency-free at runtime.

### About the type

The primary typeface is Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, designed for readers with low vision.
It ships no Vietnamese glyphs, so diacritics are filled in from Noto Sans via
`unicode-range` — the result stays seamless within a line.
